## Shape & space
4pt grid (4 8 12 16 20 24 32 40). Radius: photo cards 28, cards 24, sheets 28 (top), inputs 16, buttons/chips pill. Shadow "lilac lift": 0 8 24 rgba(108,59,209,0.12).

## Icons & motion
