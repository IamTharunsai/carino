# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Singles 21+ (core 21–35) in a launch city who are tired of swiping into silence: safety-first women, new-in-town professionals, Timeleft-style group-dinner people, hosts who already run run-clubs and dinners, and calm-tech people who've deleted apps over doom-scrolling. They open Cariño on their phone in short moments (commute, evening on the sofa) and in planning moments (before a date, on the way home).

## Product Purpose
A social + dating app that ends in real plans. An unlimited Feed (reels, moments, follows) for fun; a finite, face-checked Dates deck (10 hearts a day free) for meeting people; Plans, Tables and tickets to actually go out; safety built in and never paid for. Success = people meet in person, feel safe, and come back to say "home safe".

## Brand Personality
Warm, playful, safe. A sweet friend who's a bit cheeky, always on your side. Cute details and emojis, gentle humour, romantic words (hearts, crushes, Flame, "It's mutual 💞"), never cold or corporate. Safety is spoken softly but always visible.

## Anti-references
- Tinder/Bumble: infinite swiping, red-alert urgency, paywalled likes, fake "someone likes you!".
- LinkedIn-style seriousness ("real identity + video" must not feel like a job interview).
- Dark-pattern gamification: guilt streaks, countdown pressure, badge spam.
- Generic AI app look: cream backgrounds, glass everywhere, identical icon-card grids.

## Design Principles
1. Faces first, then words: people and their photos lead every screen.
2. Every action ends somewhere real: a chat, a plan, a booking, a check-in.
3. Cute, never childish: small soft icons, emoji with purpose, rounded everything.
4. Safety is warm: protective features look like care, not like a police station.
5. Honest by design: no fake counts, no fake urgency, prices and rules in plain words.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA: 4.5:1 body text, 44pt touch targets, full Reduce Motion support, colour never the only signal (icons + words), Dynamic Type up to 200%, inclusive gender/orientation language, captions on video.
