# Cariño Venue Partner Kit — Columbus pilot

For whoever signs venues (founder, first hires, ambassadors). Goal: sign
**20–30 founding venues** in Columbus before the pilot opens, so Events,
Tonight Near Me and "Make it a plan" are full on day one.

> Honesty rule: Cariño is pre-launch. Never quote member numbers, booking
> volumes or revenue we don't have. Use the research facts and pilot
> commitments below; collect real numbers during the pilot.

**Commercial terms marked (proposal) are for the founder to confirm.**

---

## 1. The offer in one line

> "Cariño sends you verified 21+ people who've already decided to go out —
> as couples, double dates and small groups — and we don't charge you during
> the pilot."

### Founding Venue Partner programme (proposal)
| What the venue gets | What we ask |
|---|---|
| Free listing in **Events near you** and **Tonight near me** for the pilot (6 months) | List at least 2 date-friendly events or "open tables" a month |
| "Make it a plan" button on every listing → couples and groups plan *your* venue | Honour a simple founding offer for Cariño guests (e.g. a welcome drink or reserved table) |
| **Verified guests only** — every member passed ID + liveness + photo match | Name a contact for safety escalations |
| Founding Partner badge + feature in launch content | Allow a 5-minute photo/video visit for the listing |
| Monthly report: plan views, plans made, check-ins (aggregated, no personal data) | Give feedback once a month |
| After pilot: per-booking or ticket revenue share; founding partners keep a lower rate (proposal) | — |

### Who we sign (and who we don't)
**Yes:** cafés, cocktail/wine bars, jazz and music venues, restaurants with
bar seating, board-game cafés, bowling/mini-golf, pottery/art studios,
comedy clubs, run clubs, markets, galleries.
**No:** anything adult/explicit, hourly-room hotels, venues unable to
check ID for alcohol, private homes. (App Store bans hookup/prostitution
facilitation; our safety model requires public places.)

## 2. Persona cards

**Owner / General Manager (restaurant or bar)** — economic buyer
- Goals: fill quiet nights (Mon–Thu), higher spend per cover, regulars.
- Pains: empty early-week tables; discount apps that attract one-time deal hunters; no-shows.
- Top objections: "Another app?", "What does it cost?", "Will it bring the wrong crowd?"
- Judges by: covers on slow nights, average check, effort for staff.
- Angle: **"Fill your Tuesday with couples and small groups who already planned to come."**

**Events / Marketing Manager (music venue, comedy club, studio)** — champion
- Goals: ticket sales, new audiences, social buzz.
- Pains: paid social is expensive; audiences skew the same crowd.
- Objections: "We're already on Eventbrite/Instagram.", "Show me numbers."
- Angle: **"Your event becomes a date: people invite a match or a friend group in one tap."**

**Studio / Class Owner (pottery, dance, run club)** — owner-operator
- Goals: fill beginner classes, convert first-timers to members.
- Objections: "My classes are small.", "I don't want singles hitting on students."
- Angle: **"Pairs and small groups book together — and every guest is ID-verified."**

**Hotel / Restaurant group (later phase)** — economic + legal sign-off
- Only via booking partners (OpenTable/Booking.com links); no direct room deals in the pilot.

## 5. Demo script (15 minutes, on a phone)

1. **Context (2 min)** — "You said Tuesdays are slow and you rely on Instagram. Let me show how a Tuesday plan gets made."
2. **Events near you (3 min)** — Plans tab → *Events near you*: filter by category, "Step-free venues only", "(match) is going". Ask: *"Which of your events would go here first?"*
3. **Make it a plan (3 min)** — tap **Make it a plan** → pick a match → plan detail with time and public-place note. Point out the **Date check-in** (trusted contact) — owners like that guests feel safe.
4. **Tonight near me + Host a plan (3 min)** — show open tables and a hosted plan; explain hosts bring small groups.
5. **Group hangout (2 min)** — 2+2 double date: "groups spend more and come back."
6. **Close (2 min)** — recap their pain → offer → ask for the setup slot.

Use the demo build (`USE_MOCK_BACKEND=true`) — never show real members.

## 6. Discovery questions
- Which nights or time slots are hardest to fill?
- Where do new customers find you today? What does it cost you?
- Do you get couples / small groups now? What do they spend vs walk-ins?
- What would make a guest a "bad fit" for your venue?
- Who else needs to say yes (owner, GM, events lead)?
- If this works, what would you want to see after 3 months?

## 7. Objection handling (quick reference)

| Objection | Real concern | Response | Proof | Follow-up question |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| "Another app?" | Staff effort | "Nothing to install for staff — you give us 2 events a month; we do the listing." | 15-min setup | "Which 2 events would you start with?" |
| "What does it cost?" | Hidden fees later | "Free for the pilot, no contract. Founding partners get the lowest rate after." | Written offer | "Can I send you the terms today?" |
| "Will it bring the wrong crowd?" | Safety, reputation | "Every member is ID-verified, 21+, plans are in public places, and members can be reported and removed." | Verification + 24 h review | "What would a bad fit look like for you?" |
| "We already use Eventbrite / Instagram" | Channel overlap | "Keep them — we add people who are actively planning a date or group night, not scrolling." | "Make it a plan" flow | "Which nights do those channels not fill?" |
| "Show me numbers" | Risk | "Fair — we're launching. That's why it's free; you'll get a monthly report with real plan and check-in counts." | Monthly report | "What number would make this worth keeping?" |
| "Singles hitting on my students/staff" | Guest behaviour | "Plans are mutual and pre-arranged; report/block is one tap; your contact can escalate to our safety team." | Safety centre | "Want a staff contact line?" |
| "Dating apps are cheesy for our brand" | Brand fit | "Cariño is social first — friends, groups, events — with a separate 21+ Dates tab." | Group hangouts, guilds | "Would a friends/group listing feel better first?" |

## 9. Qualification & pipeline

**Qualify (all three):** public, date/group-friendly venue · someone who
can approve a listing · willing to list ≥ 2 events/month.

**Stages:** Prospect → Contacted → Meeting set → Demo done → Agreed →
Listed → Active (≥ 1 plan/month).

**Tracker columns:** venue, category, neighbourhood, contact, role,
stage, next step + date, events committed, offer, notes, owner.

**Pilot metrics to collect per venue (aggregate only):** listing views,
plans made, check-ins ("I'm here"), repeat plans, partner NPS.
