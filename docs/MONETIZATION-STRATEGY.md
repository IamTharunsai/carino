# Cariño — Premium, pricing, conversion and reviews (Nov 15, 2026)

Written for the founder and anyone deciding pricing. Prices are US App Store
prices and vary by account, region and plan length — check the stores before
publishing. Sources at the end.

## 1. Will people pay? (honest answer)
**Not much in month one — and that's fine.** People pay a dating app when it
already works for them: enough real people nearby and at least one good
conversation. In a one-city pilot the first money comes from **Decompress
bookings (the venue pays the 12% commission)** and **founding-member annual
plans**, not monthly subscriptions. Subscriptions grow with density.

The market is moving our way: in Q2 2026 Match Group's payers fell 6% (Tinder
−5%) while **Hinge — the "intentional, meet in person" app — grew payers 17%**,
and Bumble's paying users fell ~16%. People still pay; they're paying for
outcomes, not for swiping. Cariño's pitch (verified people, real plans, safety
built in) sits exactly where the money is moving.

## 2. What competitors charge and lock (US, late 2026)
| App | Tiers | Typical price | What's behind the paywall |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tinder | Plus · Gold · (Platinum) | Plus ~$9.99 · Gold ~$13.99–24.99 | See who likes you (Gold), unlimited likes, rewind, passport, boosts |
| Bumble | Boost · Premium | Premium ~$17.99–32.99 | Beeline (who liked you), rematch, advanced filters, travel |
| Hinge | Hinge+ · HingeX | ~$29.99 · ~$49.99 /mo | All likes, unlimited likes, filters; X adds priority + "skip the line" |
| Timeleft | Subscription (events) | ~$19.99–62.99 per plan | Unlimited dinner/drinks bookings |
| Revenue per paying user | Tinder ~$17.90 · Match avg $21.13 · Grindr ~$26.51 · Bumble ~$27.65 (Q1–Q2 2026) |

**What reviewers hate (and we must never copy):**
- "Free" app, but every like is hidden. Bot-filled "who liked you" lists.
- Stacked fees: a subscription, then more per-item fees on top.
- Hard cancellation, renewals after cancelling, refused refunds.
- Proposed class action against Match over paid add-ons; Groundwork's
  "Swipe Right to Pay" report on dating paywalls.

## 3. What Cariño Plus includes today (from the code)
Paywall rows: see who liked you (free = count only), read receipts, priority
Spark windows, advanced reciprocal filters; elsewhere: 5 Super Likes/day vs 1,
Late-Night question deck, Plus filters. Price isn't set (store decides).

**Problem:** that bundle is the same as Tinder Gold-lite. "Read receipts" and
"priority windows" are weak reasons to pay; "count only" likes is the exact
pattern reviewers hate.

## 4. Recommended plans
**Free — generous, because trust is the product**
Verification, Daily Drop, unlimited messaging, plans + check-in safety, comfort
rules, Decompress booking, core games, Stage, circles, 1 Super Like a day,
undo, incognito, **and one revealed like per day ("Today's like")** instead of
a blurred wall. Safety is never paid.

**Cariño Plus — $14.99/month · $34.99/3 months · $89.99/year (~$7.50/mo)**
Sell outcomes, not visibility:
- See everyone who liked you (verified humans only — no bot padding).
- 5 Super Likes a day + a note on every like.
- Advanced filters (exact intention, languages, pace, availability, step-free).
- **Date Concierge**: AI drafts a plan both people fit (times, comfort rules,
  budget) and books Decompress partners — the human still sends and confirms.
- Priority seats at Spark Nights and small group events.
- All game packs (Late-Night deck, couples-to-be decks).
- Travel: Worldwide discovery before you arrive.

**Cariño Together — $4.99/month per couple (new idea)**
Dating apps lose customers when they succeed. Couples mode keeps them:
shared calendar, date-night bookings with partner perks, memories, Phone-Down
nights. You earn from success instead of from loneliness.

**À la carte (store billing, clearly priced, never required)**
Gifts on Stage, extra Super Likes. **No paid "boosts"** — pay-to-be-seen undermines
"everyone here is real and equal" and draws the same anger reviewers aim at
Tinder/Bumble.

**Founding members (real scarcity, real number)**
The first 1,000 verified members in a city: Plus free for 3 months, then a
lifetime price lock at $9.99/mo. The counter shows the true remaining number.

**Price rules:** one price for everyone (no age-based pricing — it has drawn
lawsuits); annual shown as the per-month figure next to monthly; 3-month as the
middle option; renewal price shown before purchase; cancel in two taps.

## 5. Turning free users into paying ones — ethical psychology
| Principle | Where it shows up in Cariño | Line we don't cross |
|---|---|---|
| Show the paywall at the moment of desire | When someone likes you, when a filter would help, when booking a date | Never on first launch, never mid-conversation |
| Reciprocity | Give real value free first: verification, plans, safety | No "free" label on a locked app |
| Endowment | A **free Plus week after your first real plan** — no card needed, no auto-charge | No trials that silently convert |
| Anchoring | Annual per-month price beside monthly; 3-month middle | No fake "was $59.99" strike-throughs |
| Social proof | Real numbers only ("312 verified people in Columbus") | No invented counts, likes or activity |
| Goal gradient | Profile aura progress, streaks that forgive a missed day | No guilt copy, no "your likes will expire!" |
| Commitment / identity | "Founding member" badge, chosen tone in notifications | No lock-in, cancel any time |
| Emotion | The feeling we sell is **hope + safety + belonging** ("people who actually show up") | Never sell loneliness or fear of missing out |

## 6. More reviews — and better ones
1. **Ask at happy moments only**, using Apple's and Google's own review prompts
   (`in_app_review` package):
   - a mutual match
   - a plan checked in "home safe" and marked worthwhile
   - a "fully present" Phone-Down score
   - a 7-day streak

   At most once every 60 days, and never in the first 3 sessions.
2. **Don't filter who gets asked.** Google forbids asking "Do you like the app?"
   before the prompt, and Apple only allows its own prompt. Instead, keep a
   permanent **"Send feedback"** row in You, so unhappy people reach us first.
3. **Reply to every 1–3★ review within 24 h** in App Store Connect and the Play
   Console. Fix the top complaint and say so in release notes.
4. **Remove review-killers before launch:**
   - hidden fees
   - hard cancellation
   - bots (our ID check is the moat)
   - push spam (our caps and quiet hours)
   - paywalled safety
5. **Never incentivise reviews.** Both stores ban it.
6. Real-world word of mouth: **launch nights with venue partners**, plus the
   referral programme (verified friends only).
