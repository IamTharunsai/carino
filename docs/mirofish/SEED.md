# Cariño — seed brief for audience simulation

**What it is.** Cariño is a social app for adults (21+ for dating) where
every member is ID-verified (government ID + liveness + photo match). It
combines private chats and friend groups, a short-video "Stage" feed that
ends ("You're caught up") and can be steered in plain words, and a 21+
Dates tab. The goal of the app is real-life plans, not screen time.

**Launch city.** Columbus, Ohio. Pilot with a city waitlist.

**Key features.**
- Verified humans only; AI-generated content always labelled.
- Dates: stated intentions (Tonight, Casual, Long-term, Hangout & Play),
  mutual-fit matching (both people must want to meet each other), video
  intros, like with a note/gift/Spark, games before chat, Spark video calls
  (both accept, never anonymous), Scam Shield warnings about money requests.
- Social: circles (Inner circle, Friends, Dates, everyone), group chats with
  a Plan button and votes, events, group hangouts, micro-hosted plans,
  guilds, coffee chats for professionals.
- Relationships: Honest Status (married/in a relationship pauses dating),
  Couples mode with shared calendar, memories and booking links.
- Privacy & safety: app lock (Face/fingerprint), hidden chats, Vanish chats
  with screenshot blocking on Android, report/block everywhere, 24-hour
  human review, safety tools never paywalled, date check-in with a trusted
  contact.

**Business model.** Free tier is complete; Cariño Plus subscription (test
price $12.99/month; founding offer under consideration) adds who-liked-you,
advanced filters, extra Sparks. Referral: give a week of Plus, get a week
(verified friends only). No ads in chats; no tracking-based ads.

**Competitors.** Tinder, Hinge, Bumble, Feeld, Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp,
event apps (Timeleft, Partiful).

**Known risks.** Verification friction at signup; cold start in one city;
Apple treats dating as a saturated category; the "Tonight" intention could
be read as a hookup feature.
